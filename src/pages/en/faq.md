## What is this app?

A map and a list of every bus stop in Heraklion. You can search for a stop by name or code, find the stops near you and see how many minutes until the next bus arrives.

## Is this the official app of Astiko KTEL Irakleiou?

No. This app has no association with [Astiko KTEL Irakleiou](https://astiko-irakleiou.gr/) (Αστικό ΚΤΕΛ Ηρακλείου), Heraklion's city bus operator, and does not represent it. For official information, timetables, tickets and announcements, please visit their website.

## Why was it made?

It's a hobby project, built to solve an everyday problem: quickly checking on your phone when the next bus will reach your stop. It's free and has no ads.

## Where does the data come from?

The list of stops and the lines serving them is stored in the app. Arrival times come in real time from the public citybus data service (rest.citybus.gr).

## How accurate are the arrival times?

They are estimates from the live data service and refresh automatically every 30 seconds while the page is open. Traffic delays or timetable changes may not show up immediately, so it's a good idea to be at the stop a little early.

## Why are no arrivals shown for a stop?

If no bus is expected soon, you'll see "No buses expected right now". If the live data service is unavailable, you'll see an error message with a button to try again.

## How does "Near me" work?

When you press the button, your browser asks for permission to use your location. If you allow it, the app shows the 5 nearest stops with their straight-line distance. The nearest stop may be across the street, serving the opposite direction, which is why more than one is shown.

Your location is never sent anywhere and is not stored. More in the [privacy policy](/en/privacy/).

## Can I install it as an app?

Yes. HopOn can go on your phone's home screen and open full screen like a regular app, without downloading anything from an app store.

- **iPhone (Safari):** tap the Share button, then "Add to Home Screen".
- **Android (Chrome):** open the ⋮ menu and tap "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".

Pages you opened recently also open without a connection. Arrival times always need a connection. On iPhone, installing it also helps keep your favourite stops from being cleared.

## I found a mistake in a stop or a line. What's correct?

The app's data may not always be up to date. The official source for stops, lines and timetables is always the [Astiko KTEL Irakleiou](https://astiko-irakleiou.gr/) website.
