This policy describes how the "Heraklion Bus Stops" application (the "App") processes personal data in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (General Data Protection Regulation, "GDPR") and Greek Law 4624/2019.

The App is an unofficial, non-commercial hobby project and is not affiliated with Astiko KTEL Irakleiou. It does not require an account, does not display advertising and does not use cookies. It measures traffic with anonymous, aggregated statistics (section 2.4).

## 1. Data controller

The data controller is Sakellaris Sfakiotakis, who develops and operates the App as a private individual.

Contact for data protection matters: [site-mail](mailto:heraklionbusstops@sakellaris.mozmail.com)

Given the limited scope of the processing, no Data Protection Officer (DPO) has been appointed, as none is required under Article 37 GDPR.

## 2. Data we process

### 2.1 Technical connection data (server logs)

When you access the App, the hosting provider automatically records technical data sent by your browser, such as your IP address, the date and time of the request, the page requested and your browser type (user agent).

- **Purpose:** the technical operation and security of the App and the investigation of errors.
- **Legal basis:** the controller's legitimate interest in operating the App securely and reliably (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).
- **Retention:** this data is kept for a limited period in accordance with the hosting provider's retention settings and is then deleted automatically. It is not combined with other data and is not used to identify visitors.

### 2.2 Geolocation ("Near me" feature)

The "Near me" feature uses your device's location only if you press the corresponding button and grant permission through your browser.

- **Purpose:** finding and displaying the nearest bus stops.
- **Legal basis:** your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you may withdraw at any time in your browser settings.
- **How it is processed:** your location is processed solely on your device. It is not sent to the App's server or to any third party, it is not stored, and it is discarded as soon as you reload or close the page. The controller never has access to it.

### 2.3 Bus arrival times

To display arrival times, the App's server sends a request to the citybus live data service (rest.citybus.gr). The same applies to the bus positions shown on the map. The request contains only the stop or line code and the display language. No personal data, including your IP address, is transmitted to citybus.

### 2.4 Visitor statistics (Vercel Web Analytics)

The App uses Vercel Web Analytics to measure traffic. For each page view, it records the page visited, the page you came from (referrer), and your country, device type, operating system and browser.

- **Purpose:** understanding how the App is used (for example, which pages are used most) and improving it.
- **Legal basis:** the controller's legitimate interest in improving the App (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).
- **How it is processed:** the service does not use cookies and stores nothing on your device. Visitors are told apart using a cryptographic hash of the request, which is deleted automatically after 24 hours. Your IP address is not stored, and the controller only sees aggregated data that cannot identify individual visitors.

## 3. Recipients

**Vercel Inc.** (440 N Barranca Ave #4133, Covina, CA 91723, USA) provides hosting services for the App and processes the data described in sections 2.1 and 2.4 as a processor on behalf of the controller, in accordance with Article 28 GDPR and Vercel's [Data Processing Addendum](https://vercel.com/legal/dpa). See also [Vercel's privacy policy](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy).

**OpenStreetMap Foundation** (St John's Innovation Centre, Cowley Road, Cambridge, CB4 0WS, United Kingdom). Map tiles are loaded directly from OpenStreetMap's servers, so your browser transmits your IP address and technical information to them, as with any content loaded from a third-party website. Embedding the map is based on the legitimate interest in providing the App's core functionality (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). The OpenStreetMap Foundation processes this data as an independent controller under its own [privacy policy](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy).

Data is not sold, rented or disclosed to any other third party, unless required by law.

## 4. Transfers outside the EU

- **Vercel (USA):** transfers are based on the safeguards in Vercel's Data Processing Addendum, in particular the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (Articles 45 and 46 GDPR).
- **OpenStreetMap Foundation (United Kingdom):** transfers are based on the European Commission's adequacy decision for the United Kingdom (Article 45 GDPR).

## 5. Cookies and local storage

The App does not use cookies, local storage or similar technologies that store information on your device. This also applies to the visitor statistics described in section 2.4. For this reason, no cookie consent banner is shown.

## 6. Automated decision-making

No decisions are made based solely on automated processing, including profiling (Article 22 GDPR).

## 7. Your rights

Under Articles 15 to 21 GDPR, you have the right to:

- access your data (Article 15),
- rectification (Article 16),
- erasure (Article 17),
- restriction of processing (Article 18),
- data portability (Article 20),
- object to processing based on legitimate interest (Article 21),
- withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before its withdrawal (Article 7(3)).

To exercise your rights, contact [site-mail](mailto:heraklionbusstops@sakellaris.mozmail.com). You will receive a response within one month (Article 12(3)). Please note that, because the App does not collect identifying information, it may not be possible to link technical data to a specific person (Article 11 GDPR).

## 8. Right to lodge a complaint

You have the right to lodge a complaint with the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (Kifisias 1-3, 115 23 Athens, Greece, [www.dpa.gr](https://www.dpa.gr)) or with the supervisory authority of the EU member state where you live or work.

## 9. Security

All communication with the App takes place exclusively over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). The App collects only the minimum data necessary (data minimisation principle, Article 5(1)(c) GDPR).

## 10. Children

The App does not knowingly collect data from children and does not require any identifying information.

## 11. Changes to this policy

The controller may amend this policy when the way the App works or the legal framework changes. The current version is always published on this page, together with its effective date.
